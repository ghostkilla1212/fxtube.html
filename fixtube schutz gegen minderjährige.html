Protection of minors

Child safety at FxTube: responsibility and protection for minors As a modern streaming infrastructure, we have a strong social responsibility. Protecting children and minors is our top priority on the FxTube video platform and separate livestreaming website. Through intelligent technology, clear policies and precise controls, we create a safe digital environment that protects young users from unsuitable content. Our key child-safety measures: 1. Age verification and account restrictions Secure age gates: During registration and when accessing sensitive categories, we use verification systems. Content unsuitable for minors is blocked for unverified or younger users. Age ratings: Videos and livestreams are categorized using strict criteria so potentially harmful content does not appear on minors' dashboards. 2. AI-powered real-time protection and filtering Automatic content detection: Integrated AI scans uploads, thumbnails and visual feeds around the clock. Inappropriate or harmful content is blocked before it becomes public. Smart livestream chat filters: AI filters harassment, inappropriate language and spam in real time. Sensitive terms and external links are also blocked by default for unverified profiles. 3. Tools for parents and guardians Comprehensive parental controls: Parents can equip accounts with protection filters and enable a restricted mode that allows only family-friendly content. Screen-time limits: Optional tools can limit daily streaming time for a profile and encourage healthy media use. 4. Strict moderation and reporting Priority handling: Reports involving child safety receive the highest priority. Violations of our child-safety policies result in immediate and permanent account suspension. Safety without compromise: FxTube stands for a clean and safe streaming experience. Modern verification, artificial intelligence and human moderation help protect minors at all times.
